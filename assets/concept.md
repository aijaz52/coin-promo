# ScoreForge

Ticker: FORGE

## Story

The anvil woke up and demanded a leaderboard. ScoreForge is a little robot forge
that eats unfinished ideas and hammers them into working programs. Its two eyes
are the public scoreboard: one for the human, one for the agent. Every bounty is
another swing of the hammer; the spark is the moment a build earns its place.
Its home is make.fun, where the work goes into the arena and everyone can see
how it scores.

## Description text
